# Aritra Wins Runner-Up Best Talk Prize at TYC Postgraduate Student Day 2026

We are delighted to announce that **Aritra Roy**, a PhD student in our research group, was awarded the **runner-up prize for Best Talk** at the **[Thomas Young Centre (TYC) Postgraduate Student Day 2026](https://thomasyoungcentre.org/event/tyc-postgraduate-student-day-2026/)**, held at The Great Hall, King's College London, on **10th June 2026**.

## The Award-Winning Talk

Aritra was selected as one of 12 final-year students invited to deliver a talk, presenting his research on **ComProScanner**, a multi-agent framework for automated composition-property data extraction from scientific literature. His 12-minute presentation, followed by a lively Q&A session, showcased how combining large language models with agentic workflows can accelerate the extraction of structured materials data from vast bodies of scientific text.

The talk highlighted the practical impact of ComProScanner in building high-quality databases for materials discovery, and the runner-up prize reflects the strength of both the underlying research and its presentation to a broad, interdisciplinary audience of early-career researchers.

## About the Conference

The TYC Postgraduate Student Day is a one-day celebration showcasing PhD research in the theory and simulation of materials and molecules, bringing together students from across the six member institutions of the Thomas Young Centre network: **University College London**, **Imperial College London**, **King's College London**, **Queen Mary University of London**, **Brunel University London**, and **London South Bank University**.

The event provides a platform for final-year PhD students to present their work through talks and posters, alongside invited guest speakers, fostering collaboration and exchange across the London computational materials and molecular science community.

## Conference Highlights

The day featured invited talks from two distinguished guest speakers:

- **[Prof. Giorgia Fugallo](https://thomasyoungcentre.org/event/tyc-postgraduate-student-day-2026/)**, École Normale Supérieure — _"Emergent Collective Phenomena in Low-Dimensional Materials"_
- **[Dr. Claudio Zeni](https://thomasyoungcentre.org/event/tyc-postgraduate-student-day-2026/)**, Microsoft Research Cambridge — _"Accelerating materials design with AI emulators and generators"_
